Job market paper

The Impact of Weather on U.S. Agricultural Capacity Utilization: A Multiple-Output Nonparametric Analysis

A study of weather and agricultural capacity using state-level data, 1960–2015.

Read paper ↗
Research summary

A nonparametric production model estimates capacity at the minimum short-run ray average cost, allowing for multiple agricultural outputs and fixed and variable inputs. Most states operate below full capacity. A second-stage model relates the capacity shortfall to weather conditions.

With Stephen L. Ross
Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)

Research summary

The paper distinguishes schools’ offers of in-person instruction from students’ actual attendance. It examines learning outcomes across instructional modes, including the challenges of synchronous hybrid teaching.

Working paper

Generalized Leontief Cost Function Analysis of U.S. Ambulatory Care Industry

Input substitution and labor–IT complementarity in U.S. ambulatory care, 1987–2019.

Research summary

A Generalized Leontief cost function estimates substitution between inputs and input-demand price elasticities. The analysis finds increasing complementarity between labor and IT/software capital.
