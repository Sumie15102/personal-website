Shangyue Jiang

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Economics at the University of Connecticut. My research interests are production economics and microeconomic theory.

My work examines how information technology changes the substitution between high-skilled and lower-skilled labor, and how factors outside a firm’s control shape its production decisions and outcomes.
